Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

You can change your cookie choice at any time.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let a site remember things about your visit, like your preferences or, in our case, anonymised analytics data.

2. Cookies We Use

This site keeps things minimal. We only use cookies for analytics — never for advertising, cross-site tracking, or selling data.

CookiePurposeDuration

3. No Advertising Cookies

We do not run ad networks, retargeting pixels, or third-party marketing cookies on this site. Some outbound links are affiliate links — see our Privacy Policy for how those work.

4. Your Choices

When you first visit, you can Accept or Decline analytics cookies. Declining disables Google Analytics for your session. You can change your mind anytime using the "Manage preferences" button above, or by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies directly in their settings.

5. Third-Party Cookies

If you click an affiliate or outbound link, the destination site may set its own cookies once you land there. Those are governed by that site's own cookie policy — this policy only covers mcjohnson.website itself.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
